How it works

The whole idea in one minute.

1

A hub forms

A stock's 4-hour candles keep closing inside a tight range for at least 4 days. It's resting, or "coiling".

2

A candle closes outside

The first 4-hour candle that closes above or below the range is the breakout. Above is bullish, below is bearish.

3

It heads to the next shelf

Earlier ranges act as shelves. The nearest one is the target. If a candle closes back inside the range, the breakout failed.
