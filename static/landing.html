FROM HUB TO ALERT

Four steps. One clear plan.

A hub is where orders pile up. We watch it — and tap you only when the rules say so.

01

A hub forms

4-hour closes hold inside a tight range for at least 4 four-hour candles. The stock is coiling, and there's no trade yet.

02

A candle closes outside

The first 4-hour close beyond the hub is the breakout, never the wick. A wick through the edge is not a setup.

03

The retest is the entry

Price pulls back to the hub edge within five 4-hour candles. If the close holds the edge, that's the entry — the shelf above is the target, and the edge is the kill price.

THREE SETUPS, NOT THIRTY

One playbook. Three measured setups.

Everything else is watch-only until the Lab measures it. These three have a graded record behind them.

Hub breakout

A retest · +0.50% a trade · 320 trades

The breakout is a heads-up. The retest is the entry. The shelf above is the target. Room 1.5× or more, long pattern, 1 ATR stop.

Double bottom

A retest · +0.30% a trade · 239 trades

Two lows, a neckline, and a 4:00 PM retest close. The entry isn't taken past halfway to the target.

Gap fade & rip

Post-earnings · +2.77% a trade · 341 trades

A stock gaps after the print, fades, then rips through the gap-day high. The one breakout pattern that is positive on its own.

THE DISCIPLINE

NextShelf actually forces you to wait.

Most tools sell you speed. This one sells you the pause — because the pause is where the edge is.

Two checks a day

The 1:30 PM verdict candle and the 4:00 PM confirm. Nothing else needs your eyes. If nothing is setting up, the honest answer is to do nothing.

Never hold through the print

Retail options traders lose 5–9% around earnings. The exit is written before the entry, and the print is a hard stop — after it is a separate trade.

The retest, not the breakout

Buying the breakout is the old rule, and the Lab ruled it out. The entry comes on the pullback to the edge — and only if the close holds it.

QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Is it a signal?

No. It's a tap on the shoulder — hey, check that setup, something is happening. The card shows the plan; the trade stays yours.

Do I need to watch the screen?

No. Two checks a day: the 1:30 PM verdict candle and the 4:00 PM confirm. The alerts reach you by Telegram, text, or call.

What if the breakout already happened?

Then you wait for the retest. The breakout is a heads-up, not an entry — the entry comes on the pullback to the edge, and only if the close holds it.

Can I hold through earnings?

NextShelf won't let the alert fire into a print. The exit is before the earnings — holding through it is a separate bet, and retail loses 5–9% around earnings.
